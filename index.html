About RL Café & Tea

RL Café & Tea opened in 2026, run by Ronaldo S. Lacuna and a partner barista. We are a neighborhood café serving espresso and brewed coffee, milk teas, pastries and light meals for dine-in, take-out and pick-up. Whether you are a student looking a quiet corner, an office worker grabbing a quick pick-up, or a group of friends and family celebrating together, there is a seat for you.
